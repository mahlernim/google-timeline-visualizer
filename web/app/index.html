How to export Timeline on iPhone
  1. Open Google Maps and tap your profile picture.
  2. Open Settings, then Personal content.
  3. Select Export Timeline data.
  4. Save Timeline.json in the Files app, then return here.

To keep this app on your iPhone, use Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
