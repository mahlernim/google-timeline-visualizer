Bring a year of travel to life.

Timeline Visualizer turns a Google Maps Timeline export into an animated map video on your Android phone. Choose a year or month range, preview the route, and create an MP4 that is ready to watch or share.

Create your story
• Load a Timeline.json export from Android or iPhone
• Select a whole year or a custom range of months
• Personalize a reusable title such as “2026 Mina's Timeline”
• Preview the animation before creating the full-quality video
• Choose a 15, 30, 60, or 90-second video

Keep your videos together
Completed videos appear in Creations with a thumbnail, title, date, duration, and Timeline period. You can also add older MP4 files, then watch or share them from one place.

Designed for privacy
Your Timeline.json, route list, titles, and generated videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. No Google sign-in, analytics, advertising, or location permission is used.

To draw the map, the app requests map tiles from CARTO. Those requests include map-area tile coordinates and normal network information, which may reveal places in your Timeline to CARTO. The app explains this before the first Timeline is loaded and lets you cancel.
