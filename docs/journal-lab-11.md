# Journal Lab 11

This side build introduces a simpler three-step Travel Journal welcome flow and a persistent local route projection.

- Trip, recap, and custom video choices appear as soon as Journal metadata is ready.
- Prepared journeys are cached locally, so the app does not reconstruct the full history on every launch.
- Updated Timeline imports rebuild only the affected route window.
- Journal freshness guidance is quieter and appears after 21 days.
- Timeline files and the Travel Journal remain on the device.

Journal Lab installs separately from the main Timeline Visualizer app.
