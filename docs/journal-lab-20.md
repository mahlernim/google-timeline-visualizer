# Journal Lab 20

Journal Lab 20 is an experimental refinement of the version 15 Close-up camera.

- Close-up can frame local routes more tightly, with its context floor reduced from 15 km to 6 km.
- A local stay after a transfer is treated as a meaningful arrival when its recorded route lasts at least one hour.
- The camera starts closing during the final part of the incoming transfer and continues moving through the local route. It does not freeze at arrival.
- Meaningful arrivals receive up to 3% of the video each, with all arrival adjustments limited to 8% of the video. Several long-haul trips therefore share one bounded budget.
- Dynamic, Fixed, and Steady camera timing remain unchanged.
- The app includes the v2.4.2 CARTO map authentication, cache, attribution, and privacy changes.

Journal Lab installs separately from Timeline Visualizer. Timeline files and the Travel Journal remain on the device. This build upgrades Journal Lab 19 in place.
