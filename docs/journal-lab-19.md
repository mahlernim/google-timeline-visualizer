# Journal Lab 19

Journal Lab 19 keeps the clean version 15 camera and improves the Create interface from community feedback.

- Back now sits beside the three-step indicator.
- Completed steps are selectable, and the next valid step can be opened directly.
- Continue stays at the bottom of the current step for easier one-handed use.
- Journey preview uses a familiar play or pause control directly on the map. Tapping the map also toggles playback.
- Custom preset choices are now labelled Custom settings.
- Preview continues to use the compact on-screen rendering size independently of the selected export resolution.

The Journal database, route preparation, trip discovery, saved projects, and version 15 camera behavior are unchanged.

Journal Lab installs separately from Timeline Visualizer. Timeline files and the Travel Journal remain on the device.
