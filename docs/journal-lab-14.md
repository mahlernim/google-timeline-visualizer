# Journal Lab 14

Journal Lab 14 keeps Create responsive while making route preparation and trip discovery recoverable.

- Find trips now owns its work state and returns to a usable screen after success, cancellation, or failure.
- Route requests are bounded and coordinated so newer selections cannot silently strand earlier controls.
- Saved-trip coverage is shown only when the loaded Journal route covers that trip.
- Cached route data is read by the requested period and reused across prepared periods.
- Camera pacing now follows perceived movement across the visible map. Regional travel moves quickly while close-up routes remain calmer.
- Video settings focus on map view, duration, format, resolution, and frame rate. Detailed pacing controls are automatic.

This remains a separately installable experimental build. It does not replace the production app and does not upload Timeline data.
