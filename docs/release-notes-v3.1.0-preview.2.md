# Timeline Visualizer 3.1.0 preview 2

This Android preview adds a new ending to your videos with large travel stats, a playful headline based on your recorded journey, and a short explanation.

- Read your total distance and travel mix at a glance. When activity labels are sparse, the second stat shows days with recorded movement.
- See a different punchline when your journey supports it, including late nights, early starts, weekend travel, and a standout day.
- The ending has more reading time while preserving your chosen video length. Map credits remain visible in the background.
- Supports all 11 app languages and your selected distance units.

Available for testing before a production release. Please check whether the text is readable on your phone, the headline fits your journey, and the explanation makes sense. All analysis runs locally on your device.
