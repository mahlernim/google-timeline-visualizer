# Trips Lab 6

Trips Lab 6 is the production candidate for Trips and Recaps.

- Yearly recaps use the complete calendar year.
- Automatic video titles use an optional saved name and localized short dates.
- Video cards show a compact preset, aspect ratio, and duration summary.
- Full historical settings remain available from the video menu.
- Raw videos use a fixed 100 m accuracy filter without an extra control.
- Trips and Recaps text is localized throughout the app.

This remains a separately installed test build and is not a Google Play release.
